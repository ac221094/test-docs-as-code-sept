BMC Community is a BMC-specific platform that you can use to engage with your product's customers and drive their attention to important topics. Additionally, this platform helps you to:

- Understand customer pain points through interactions
- Gather customer feedback and work on the identified improvement areas
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams in BMC
- Establish your blogging profile and enhance it over time

BMC Community offers various types of content, such as blogs, knowledge articles, documents, ideas, and events. However, the IX team primarily uses blogs to share their work with a larger audience.
