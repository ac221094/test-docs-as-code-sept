# Product overview

BMC Project Planner, a cloud-based project management application, helps teams to plan, organize, track, and collaborate on projects from any location through a web browser or mobile device. It is designed for project managers, team members, business leaders, and organizations of all sizes that need to manage tasks and deliver projects efficiently. 

By centralizing project information in the cloud, the application enables teams to stay aligned, make informed decisions, and complete projects more effectively.
